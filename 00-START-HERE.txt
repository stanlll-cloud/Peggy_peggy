RICOH GR CUSTOMIZER - START HERE

Version: 0.7.4-en.7, based on upstream 0.7.4.
Previously tested models: Ricoh GR IV and Ricoh GR IV HDF (owner-reported earlier edition).
This update has not been tested on a physical Mac or camera.

1. Open 01-USER-GUIDE-English.pdf first.
   It contains installation steps, camera settings, FAQs, and Firmware vs. Script instructions.

2. Move Ricoh GR Customizer.app to your Applications folder, then open it.
   If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

3. The interface supports Traditional Chinese and English.
   Switch languages at the bottom of the sidebar.

More color recipes: https://luts-maker.uk

Halation: every added slot includes halation. Compare Off / Weak / Strong in the app preview.
On the camera, choose Weak or Strong under the added slot's Halation parameter in Image Control.
Write the new firmware to the card and install it in the camera for the new effect to apply.
Camera and app preview use the upstream per-preset highlight threshold.

Get community updates from https://github.com/stanlll-cloud/Peggy_peggy
Download the new ZIP and replace the app manually. In-app signed installation is not configured.

This is a community beta. Read the guide before writing firmware to a card.
Complete corresponding source and license notices are included in this package.

RESTORING FACTORY FIRMWARE
Reset camera settings first: Setup > Reset > Reset to Factory Default.
Modified slots, aspect ratios, ADJ soft focus and date imprint settings may freeze factory firmware menus. The app asks for confirmation in red; Cancel is focused.

This update fixes aspect-ratio preview hangs on refused intermediate inputs. No camera limits or safety checks were relaxed.
